Terms of xsd/common/UBL-XAdESv132-2.1.xsd and xsd/common/UBL-XAdESv141-2.1.xsd

These two files are the ETSI XAdES schemas of ETSI TS 101 903 (XML Advanced
Electronic Signatures), namespaces http://uri.etsi.org/01903/v1.3.2# and
http://uri.etsi.org/01903/v1.4.1#, as modified by OASIS and published in the
OASIS UBL 2.1 package (UBL 2.1 OASIS Standard, 4 November 2013,
https://docs.oasis-open.org/ubl/os-UBL-2.1/UBL-2.1.zip). Each file starts with
the header OASIS added, which states its origin and the change made:

  UBL-XAdESv132-2.1.xsd: "This is a copy of
  http://uri.etsi.org/01903/v1.3.2/XAdES.xsd modified only to change the
  importing URI for the XML DSig schema."

  UBL-XAdESv141-2.1.xsd: "This is a copy of
  http://uri.etsi.org/01903/v1.4.1/XAdESv141.xsd modified only to change the
  importing URI for the XAdES v1.3.2 schema."

Neither these files nor the schemas ETSI publishes at those addresses carry a
copyright or licence notice. The UBL 2.1 specification describes each of them
as "provided only as a convenience for those users who choose to include an
XAdES extension inside of a digital signature".
